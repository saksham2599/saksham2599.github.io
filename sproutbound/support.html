How to play

Your sprout bounces automatically. Drag horizontally to steer, or enable tilt in Garden settings. Land near a leaf’s centre and collect nectar to charge Bloom. Tap Bloom when charged. Stomp monsters from above; hitting their sides is dangerous. Watch a trap’s warning before it snaps.

Restore Pro

Open Sproutbound Pro and choose Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account used to subscribe. Monthly and yearly Pro both remove all AdMob ads while active.

Manage or cancel

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sproutbound Pro, or choose Manage subscription in the app. Cancellation retains access through the paid period. Plans renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. No free trial.

Progress and privacy

Progress is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app can remove it. There is no game account or cloud leaderboard. Advertising privacy choices appear in Garden settings wherever required.
