Privacy policy

Sproutbound is provided by saksham goyal. This policy explains the game, its optional purchases, advertising and this support website.

Game data stays on your iPhone

Scores, progress, quests, unlocked colours and preferences are saved locally. There is no Sproutbound account or developer analytics backend. Optional motion steering is processed on your device. The game does not upload your gameplay or motion data to us.

Optional Apple Game Center

If you choose to connect Game Center, Sproutbound shares your run scores with Apple for separate Classic and Precision leaderboards. Apple associates entries with your Game Center player identity and chosen display name. Scores may be visible to other players according to your Apple settings. A small pending best-score queue is stored on this iPhone, separated by Game Center player, to retry failed uploads. Automated test scores are never submitted. Manage your Game Center identity and privacy in Apple settings. Local play does not require Game Center.

Apple purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. Sproutbound checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine Pro access and restore purchases. We do not receive payment-card details. Apple handles purchase records under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob

The free game uses Google AdMob to show occasional interstitial ads between eligible endless runs. The app updates Google’s User Messaging Platform consent information and presents required privacy choices before requesting ads. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access IDFA. Publisher first-party ID and publisher ad personalization are disabled; these settings do not mean no data is collected.

Google’s advertising services may process your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device or app-scoped identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Google and its advertising partners process data under their own policies. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

While Pro is active, Sproutbound prevents new ad requests and ad presentations. Data already processed by Google is subject to Google’s retention policies. Required privacy choices remain available in Garden settings.

Your choices and deletion

Use Privacy choices in Garden settings when available to review or withdraw advertising choices. Deleting the app removes its locally saved progress; restoring an iPhone backup may restore that data. Manage or cancel Pro separately in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Audience

Sproutbound is a general-audience casual game, not a service specifically directed to children. It has no chat, user accounts or user-generated content. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us with privacy concerns.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address and content you choose to send and use them to respond. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. This static website has no added analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may log IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Contact saksham goyal at anitagoyaldev@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or data requests. We will update the effective date when this policy changes.
