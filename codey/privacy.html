Privacy, simply.

Codey is a playful coding companion for a general audience, including families. This policy covers the public iOS release of Codey: Play, Code & Talk, published by Anita Goyal.

Effective September 7, 2026 · Version 1.0

Game progress stays on your device

Codey stores game progress, preferences, puzzle results, achievements and discovery stamps locally. There is no Codey account and no developer-operated cloud sync. We do not receive your gameplay messages, activity history or saved progress.

Your voice

Microphone access is optional and requires iOS permission. The voice playground records a short clip of up to six seconds to play it back with voice effects. Recordings are held temporarily in memory, are not uploaded or saved to an audio file, and are discarded when you delete the clip or close the playground. Recording stops when the app goes into the background. You can revoke microphone permission in iPhone Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple processes Codey Pro subscriptions and payments. Codey checks Apple's verified purchase information on the device to determine whether Pro access is active. We do not receive your card or billing details. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

The public version 1.0 does not request or display advertisements and does not send analytics or tracking events to the developer. It does not request advertising tracking permission. A separate development build contains an explicitly opened Google test-ad preview; that preview is disabled in the public release and is not part of normal gameplay.

Sharing and support

If you choose to export a progress card or share it with another service, that service's privacy practices apply. If you email support, we receive the message and the contact details you provide. We use them to respond to your request. Please do not send voice recordings or sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you may ask us to delete it.

Families and controls

Codey includes a grown-up gate for purchases and microphone access, optional break reminders and reduced-motion settings. The gate is not identity or age verification. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay.

Removing local data

You can delete a recording inside the voice playground. To remove all local Codey data, delete the app from your device rather than offloading it. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in Apple Account settings.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Questions or deletion requests about support correspondence can be sent to anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.
