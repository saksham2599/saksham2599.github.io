Codey: Play,
Code & Talk.

Care for a coding buddy, explore four mini-games, guide a robot through puzzles and make silly voice recordings.

Need a hand?

Email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please leave out sensitive information and recordings.

What is free?

Care and conversation, all four game types, the first three Command Trail puzzles, the Explorer Passport and Little Buddy, Gentle Giant and Chipmunk voices.

What does Pro include?

The optional annual subscription unlocks Robot, Space Echo and Backwards voice effects, all 12 Command Trail puzzles, and extra studio themes while your subscription is active. The App Store shows the current price for your region before you subscribe.

Manage or restore Pro

Open More → Grown-up corner → Restore purchases. To cancel, use Manage subscription in the app or iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting Codey does not cancel your subscription.

Microphone or sound trouble?

Allow microphone access for Codey in iPhone Settings. Unmute Codey and check the voice-volume setting. Close other recording apps and try a short clip. Recordings stay on the device and disappear when the voice playground closes.

Progress and devices

Progress is saved on the device. There is no Codey cloud account or progress transfer. This first release is designed for iPhone.
