Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026. Developer: Saksham Goyal. Contact: anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Shardwake iPhone game, this support website and support communications. Gameplay does not require a Shardwake account. Optional advertising and purchase features apply only in builds where they are available; the current pre-release production candidate has both disabled.

1. Information on your device

The game stores settings, high scores, run checkpoints, cosmetic progress and, where applicable, purchase-access information on your device. It also keeps a bounded local history of gameplay events for app operation. The current app does not transmit this gameplay history to the developer or provide developer-operated cloud saves.

Deleting the app removes this local information. Copies in device or iCloud backups are governed by your Apple backup settings; deleting the app does not necessarily delete those backups.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

In ad-enabled builds, choosing Watch Ad contacts Google AdMob and may display privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads are optional: declining an ad does not prevent starting a new run. Ad-disabled builds do not initialize advertising or make ad requests through the app's ad flow.

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request permission to access Apple's advertising identifier. This does not mean that advertising is free of data collection. Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), app/device identifiers, diagnostics and performance information, ad views and interactions for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google test ads also involve network requests.

When advertising is available, use Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review available choices. Ad requests depend on the consent state reported by Google's consent SDK. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google's practices and controls.

3. Purchases, where available

Apple processes iOS purchases and subscriptions. The app uses transaction and entitlement information to verify access and restore purchases; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details. The current iOS configuration uses Apple's native verification and has no developer receipt-verification server configured. Any change to these practices will be reflected in this policy before that configuration is released. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Support communications

If you email support, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as your name, device details or screenshots. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and maintain support records. Email is processed through our email provider. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of support information by emailing the contact above. We may need to verify the request and may retain records where required by law. We cannot directly delete data held independently by Apple or Google; use their privacy controls for those requests.

5. This website

This site has no developer-added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. External links and email services are governed by the relevant provider's policies.

6. Permissions and family inquiries

The current game does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health information, and does not ask for a date of birth. A parent or guardian with a question about information submitted to support by a child can contact us for assistance. Advertising availability and privacy choices may depend on applicable requirements and the app's released configuration.

7. Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Saksham Goyal at anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.
