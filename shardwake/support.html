Common questions

How do I continue an interrupted run?

Use Continue Run when an active saved run is available. Finishing a lost run starts your next attempt at level 1. Progress is stored on your device, not in a Shardwake cloud account.

Can I play horizontally?

Yes. The game supports portrait and both landscape orientations. Changing orientation pauses gameplay so you can resume when ready.

Why is a rewarded ad unavailable?

Optional rewarded ads are available only in builds where advertising is enabled, and availability depends on consent, network access and ad inventory. You can decline and start a new run. An incomplete or unavailable ad should not use a revive. Contact support if a completed reward was not applied.

The app is stuck at launch. What should I try?

Close and reopen the app, then restart your iPhone if needed. Tell support which screen is visible and your app/iOS versions. Avoid deleting the app as a first step: deletion removes local progress.

How do I manage privacy or delete local progress?

Settings includes Ad Privacy Options. Read the privacy policy for details. Deleting the app removes its on-device saves; device backups are managed separately through Apple.

What about purchases or subscriptions?

Version 1.0.0 has no purchases or subscriptions. Earn cosmetic ball colors through play. After defeat, you may choose an optional rewarded ad for one continuation per run. If no ad is available, you can always start a new run.
