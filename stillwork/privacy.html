Your work belongs to you.

This policy covers Stillwork for iPhone, developed by Saksham Goyal.

Your work stays on your device

Stillwork stores work threads, routines, preparation steps, focus sessions, parked thoughts, return notes, and preferences on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a server that receives this work content and do not share it for advertising. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

You choose where exported backups are saved or shared. Importing a backup replaces the local workspace after confirmation. Deleting the app removes local storage but does not remove exports or copies in backups. Archiving a work thread keeps its history.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and manages billing. Stillwork uses verified StoreKit purchase information to determine Pro access. We do not receive your payment card details. Restore purchases and subscription management are available in the app.

Advertising

Production advertising is disabled in the initial App Store release. Google advertising and consent SDKs are included but are not initialized for ad requests in that release.

If advertising is enabled in a future release, the free app may show a non-personalized Google AdMob banner on Today, outside focus sessions. Pro suppresses advertisements. We do not request tracking permission or pass work titles, notes, routines, or history as targeting data.

Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Google may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Regional consent choices will be available through Settings → Advertising privacy choices. Ads will be requested only when the consent system permits them. The app disclosures and this policy will be updated before enabling production advertising.

See Google’s privacy policy and information about partner apps.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled locally, are optional, and do not include your work titles or notes. Change permission in iPhone Settings. File import and export use Apple's system interface at your request.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate the website. We do not add analytics or advertising scripts to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Audience and contact

Stillwork is a general productivity app and is not part of Apple's Kids Category. For support or privacy questions, email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be reflected in the effective date.
