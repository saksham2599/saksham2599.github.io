Stillwork support.

Contact

Email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com with your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the issue. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or private work notes.

Restore Pro

Open Settings → Explore Stillwork Pro → Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. The first reusable routine remains free.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Stillwork. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

Save or move your work

Use Export in Stillwork Settings to save a backup. Importing a backup replaces the current workspace after confirmation, so export your current workspace first.

Remove data

Stillwork has no account or developer-hosted workspace. Deleting the app removes its local data; remove exported copies and device backups separately where you keep them. Archiving work keeps its history.

Timer and reminders

Paused sessions can be resumed after relaunch. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings if you want local end-of-session reminders. System notification settings can affect delivery.
