Privacy policy

Your space, explained.

Gravity Playground · Effective September 8, 2026

Saksham Goyal develops Gravity Playground. This policy explains how this game and its support pages handle information.

Game progress stays on your device

Scores, completed flights, earned stardust, cosmetic unlocks, and preferences are stored locally. The game has no account system or online save synchronization. An unreadable save may be retained as a local recovery copy so it is not silently lost.

Your Apple device backup settings may include local progress. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a backup may restore it. There is no separate online game account to delete.

Advertising and subscriptions

Earlier builds (1 and 2) have no advertising SDK or in-app purchases. The upcoming build 3 adds optional rewarded-video and Gravity Pro features. Production advertising and new subscriptions are currently disabled pending release configuration and provider checks. Local development testing uses test ads and simulated purchases, without real charges.

When enabled, optional videos use Google AdMob. The game requests child-directed, General-audience, non-personalized advertising, disables publisher first-party identifiers and personalization, and does not request tracking permission. These controls do not make advertising data-free: Google can process technical information such as an IP address, device and app information, advertising interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure service performance. We do not request precise location or send your scores or saved progress to an advertising server. Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

An optional video requires a grown-up check. A continue is granted only after the ad provider confirms the reward. Retrying and core gameplay remain free if no video is available. Advertising privacy choices appear in Settings when the provider requires them. The app tags requests as under the age of consent; the grown-up check is not consent to tracking or personal data collection.

Gravity Pro is an optional automatically renewing monthly or yearly Apple subscription. Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive your card details. StoreKit provides verified purchase and entitlement information so the app can provide benefits, restore active subscriptions, and remove subscription access after expiry or refund. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it. The app has no developer analytics service, account system or online progression server.

Children and family controls

The games are intended for children and adults. The family update requires a local grown-up check before external links. The check does not store answers or collect an age or date of birth. It is not consent to collect personal information. Parents or guardians should handle support requests and avoid sending children’s personal information.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information. Contact us if you need help with information you previously sent.

These web pages

The support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising scripts or developer analytics. GitHub may process technical request information to provide hosting under its privacy statement. External links are governed by their providers’ policies.

Updates and contact

We will update this policy when the game’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

Questions about privacy or support can be sent to Saksham Goyal at anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.
