How to play

Tap Launch journey. Hold the playfield to see the trajectory, then release to launch. Blue predicts a landing; gold indicates a precise landing. The next planet catches the ship automatically. One hit or missed landing ends the attempt.

Choose Try again after a miss, Fly it again after a clear, or Back to station to go home.

Pause, resume, and saved progress

Use Pause during a flight. Returning from the background keeps the flight paused until you choose Resume. Force-quitting the app abandons an unfinished flight. Completed results, settings, and unlocks are saved locally.

Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step: deletion removes local progress. Pocket Orbit and Gravity Playground are independent apps with separate saves. There is no account sync or cross-app progress transfer. Restoring an iPhone backup may restore a saved installation.

Sound and haptics

Check the in-game sound setting and your iPhone’s silent switch. Haptics can be adjusted separately. Decorative motion follows the iPhone’s Reduce Motion setting.
