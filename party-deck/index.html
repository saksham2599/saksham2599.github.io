Party Deck Support

Twenty games. One iPhone. Your favorite people.

Party Deck brings acting, drawing, guessing, voting, trivia, and conversation games to the same table. Players share a phone; no account is needed.

Get in touch

Email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com. Include the game name, app version, iOS version, and what happened. Screenshots help, but remove player names and private answers first. Never send passwords or payment details.

Start a game

Add your group in Players, choose teams where needed, and pick a game from Games. Tap the question-mark button inside any game for its rules. Private screens tell you who should hold the phone before revealing a word, role, or answer.

Can we play without internet?

The games use bundled prompts and local scoring. You can play without internet. Privacy messages, advertisements in ad-enabled versions, and external support links require a connection; an unavailable ad must not block a game.

What is saved?

Your lobby, settings, and party scores are stored on your device. Closing and reopening the app does not restore an unfinished game. Keep the app open for your current session; returning from the background may require resuming the timer.

Clear players or scores

Use Clear in Players to remove the roster, or Reset in Scores to reset party scores. Deleting the app removes its local app-container data. Device backups are managed separately through Apple.

Comfort comes first

Anyone can decline a prompt or dare. Agree on boundaries before playing and do not pressure anyone to share personal information or perform an uncomfortable action.

Advertising and privacy

The current beta has production advertising disabled. Ad-enabled versions are designed to show a banner in the game lobby, never over an active turn. When applicable, Privacy choices lets you revisit advertising consent. Read the privacy policy for version-specific details.
