Privacy Policy

Party Deck for iPhone
Effective September 8, 2026
Developer: Saksham Goyal

Current beta: production advertising is disabled. Google advertising and consent libraries are included in the new integration build but are not initialized in its default configuration. The advertising section below explains the intended behavior when an ad-enabled version is made available.

Game data stays on your device

Party Deck stores player names, team assignments, settings, and scores locally on your iPhone. Current-session clues, guesses, votes, and drawings are handled locally. The app has no developer-hosted account system, cloud leaderboard, or service that receives these game entries.

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. The app does not record conversations. System keyboards, dictation, and device backups are provided and controlled by Apple or your selected keyboard provider.

Advertising in ad-enabled versions

Party Deck integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When advertising is enabled, the app requests updated consent information before starting advertising. Where required, a privacy message is shown, and ads are requested only when the consent SDK permits them. A failed consent update leaves advertising off for that launch.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance data for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. Party Deck requests non-personalized ads, does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization, and does not pass your player names, game answers, drawings, or scores to advertising requests. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google may process information on servers outside your country and applies its own retention policies.

Your choices

Where required by the consent SDK, use Privacy choices on the Games screen to revisit advertising choices. Every game remains available without agreeing to personalized advertising. You can remove your roster with Clear in Players, reset scores in Scores, or delete the app to remove its local data. Manage device backups separately in Apple settings.

Support and beta testing

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. We do not add you to a marketing list. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us; records may be retained where needed for security or legal obligations.

For TestFlight builds, Apple may collect crash information, usage information, and feedback according to its TestFlight terms and privacy practices. Feedback you submit may be shared with the developer. Do not include other players' personal information without permission.

Children and personal information

Do not enter sensitive personal information into game answers or send children's personal information to support. Production advertising remains disabled while the app's intended audience and appropriate advertising treatment are being finalized.

Website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit. The pages contain no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by us. External links have their own privacy policies.
