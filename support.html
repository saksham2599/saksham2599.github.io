How can I help?

Help with network activity, connection tests, history, purchases, and Live Activities.

Contact support

Email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com with your NetBar version, iOS version, device model, connection type, and the steps that caused the issue. Please do not send passwords or private keys.

Why does the Live Activity stop changing?

iOS may suspend ordinary apps after they leave the foreground, and force-quitting stops them. NetBar keeps the last measured value and timestamp visible and marks it stale when it cannot collect a new sample.

Why does observed usage differ from my carrier?

NetBar records only traffic observed while iOS allows the app to run. It is not a carrier billing meter, router total, or per-app report. VPN and tunnel interfaces are excluded to avoid double counting.

How much data does a speed test use?

Download and upload each target at least ten seconds. Usage scales with connection speed, so a fast connection can use hundreds of megabytes. NetBar shows an additional warning before cellular testing.

Restore NetBar Pro

Open NetBar Settings, choose Restore Purchase, and use the Apple Account that originally bought NetBar Pro. If the product remains unavailable, confirm App Store purchases are allowed and try again later.

Delete local data

Open NetBar Settings and choose Delete All Local History. Removing NetBar also removes its local app-container data. Delete any exported copies separately from wherever you saved them.
