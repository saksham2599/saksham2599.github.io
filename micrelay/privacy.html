Your voice stays yours.

MicRelay processes recordings, voice effects, enhancement, and supported transcription on your device. We do not operate an audio-upload service.

Who provides MicRelay

MicRelay is provided by Saksham Goyal. For privacy questions, email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com.

Data inside the app

Recordings, titles, bookmarks, tags, transcripts, corrections, and processed copies are stored locally. Microphone access is used only for audio capture you initiate. Speech permission and Apple language assets support transcription where available. The app does not send your audio or text to a developer or third-party AI server.

No tracking or analytics

MicRelay has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer account login, or tracking system. We do not collect usage events, advertising identifiers, recordings, or transcripts through the app.

Purchases and trial time

Apple processes purchases under its own policies. MicRelay verifies StoreKit transactions locally to determine trial and Pro access. It stores the greatest observed trial timestamp in this device’s Keychain to handle basic clock rollback. We do not receive your payment card information or send trial timestamps to a backend.

Sharing and device backups

Sharing happens only when you choose an export destination. The destination then handles the exported copy under its own policies. Depending on your device settings, operating-system backups may include local app files. MicRelay does not provide a separate cloud-sync service.

Your controls

You can stop capture, revoke microphone and speech permissions in Settings, edit transcripts, and delete recordings and processed copies in Library. Deleting the app removes its app-container data; the device-only Keychain trial timestamp may remain. Delete shared copies and manage backups separately.

Contacting support

If you email support, the email and information you voluntarily include are received to answer your request. Do not include private audio, transcripts, credentials, or payment information. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information to deliver and secure the website under its privacy statement.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date. Questions can be sent to the contact above.
