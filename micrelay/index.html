Help for your next session.

Connect a speaker, record your voice, and revisit your words.

Contact support

Email anitagoyaldev@gmail.com with your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, speaker model, and the steps that caused the issue. Please avoid sending private recordings, transcripts, passwords, or purchase receipts.

Connect a speaker

Pair a compatible Bluetooth A2DP speaker in Settings. Start with low speaker volume. In Live, choose Natural, select an external output, and tap the large microphone. Keep the speaker away from the microphone to reduce feedback. Bluetooth adds delay; built-in speaker and headset HFP routes are excluded.

Record without a speaker

Open Record, choose a sound profile, activate the trial or unlock Pro, then tap the large microphone. Pause excludes paused time; Mute records silence. Finish saves the session to Library. Obtain appropriate permission before recording other people.

Funny voice effects

Choose Chipmunk, Giant, or Space Echo before starting Live. Natural returns to your ordinary voice. Live effects change speaker output while saved source audio is preserved. In Library, create a separate funny-voice copy to play or share. Effects may add processing delay and are not voice cloning.

Transcription and clearer speech

Apple transcription requires iOS or iPadOS 26 and a supported language/model. Allow any required model download and retry in the foreground. Original audio remains playable if transcription is unavailable. Voice enhancement creates a separate copy; compare it with the original because processing can soften quiet words and cannot guarantee removal of other speakers.

Three-day trial and Pro

Natural Live is free. The Pro trial lasts 72 hours from activation and does not charge automatically. Pro is a separate one-time purchase at the displayed local App Store price, with a US launch price of $5. Existing recordings and transcripts remain accessible after trial expiry.

Check your setup and trim recordings

Use the five-second Sound check to record and listen before a session. Save your setup under Presets. In Library, select Trim audio to preview a waveform range and save a separate clip while preserving the source.

Restore purchases

Open MicRelay Pro and tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that originally activated the trial or bought Pro. Restoration keeps the original trial expiry. Check your connection and retry if Apple cannot load purchases. Keep your device date and time accurate.

Interrupted recordings and deletion

Open Library after an interruption to check recovered recordings. The last unfinished segment may be incomplete. Delete recordings in Library; copies you previously shared and independent device backups must be managed separately.
