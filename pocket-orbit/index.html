An independent iPhone game

One tap. One more lap.

Switch orbits, find the opening, and chase the stars through four galaxies.

For iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Portrait play

Four galaxies to explore

Clear 12 Adventure levels on Easy, Medium, or Hard. Easy and Medium use two orbits; Hard adds a third. One collision ends the flight.

Keep chasing your best

Survival offers an open-ended run. Daily Challenge changes at 00:00 UTC and uses Medium difficulty. Earn ten flight colors through stardust and achievements. Scores stay on your device.

Choose your pace

Free play includes occasional ads between Survival runs and optional reward videos. Optional monthly or yearly ad-free subscriptions remove ads and let you claim the eligible run bonus without a video while active. Both plans offer the same benefits. You can keep playing for free without subscribing.
