How to play

Choose Adventure and a difficulty, finish the tutorial, then tap to launch. On Easy and Medium, tap to switch between two orbits. On Hard, use Inward and Outward or the left/right playfield to move between three. One hit ends the flight.

Challenges contains Survival and the Daily Challenge. Daily uses Medium difficulty and changes at 00:00 UTC. Daily scores are personal bests stored locally.

Pause, resume, and saved progress

Use Pause during a flight. Returning from the background keeps the flight paused until you choose Resume. Force-quitting the app abandons an unfinished flight. Completed results, settings, and unlocks are saved locally.

Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step: deletion removes local progress. Pocket Orbit and Gravity Playground are independent apps with separate saves. There is no account sync or cross-app progress transfer. Restoring an iPhone backup may restore a saved installation.

Sound and haptics

Check the in-game sound setting and your iPhone’s silent switch. Haptics can be adjusted separately. Decorative motion follows the iPhone’s Reduce Motion setting.

Ads and ad-free purchases

Reward videos are optional. If no video is available, keep playing; an ad failure does not take away earned progress. A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms completion.

Restore your active monthly or yearly subscription from Shop or Settings while signed in to the Apple Account that purchased it. Apple handles payments and refund requests. If restoration fails, check your connection and try again. Ad-free does not unlock achievement-gated colors or increase your score.

Use Ad privacy choices in Settings when available to review consent. Report inappropriate creative with the ad’s AdChoices or information control; you can also contact us with the advertiser name, time, and a screenshot. Never share payment details.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Use Manage subscription in the game’s Shop or Settings to open Apple’s subscription controls. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Plans automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancelling renewal keeps your benefits through the paid period. Expiration returns to free play and keeps your earned progress.

Monthly and yearly plans have the same benefits. The full localized charge and billing interval appear before purchase. Apple’s standard Terms of Use apply.
