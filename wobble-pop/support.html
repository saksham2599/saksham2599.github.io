Wobble Pop! support

Wobble Pop! is an iPhone-first balance game for iOS 18 or later. Keep Pip on the perch with Tilt or Thumb controls, collect three mastery badges per level, and play local Pass the Phone matches with 2–6 people.

Quick help

Tilt is not responding

Open Pause and choose Recalibrate, then hold the phone comfortably and make small movements. If motion access or a sensor is unavailable, the current attempt restarts automatically with Thumb control. You can choose a default control in Settings, and both controls unlock the same content.

Is the game free? How do I remove ads or restore Pro?

All 24 levels are free through normal progression. Go Pro • Remove ads offers US$10/month or US$100/year in the US storefront, with local pricing shown by the App Store. Both plans auto-renew and include ad-free play and one rescue per campaign run. Manage or cancel in App Store settings; cancellation keeps access until the paid period ends. Choose Restore Purchase with the Apple Account that subscribed or bought the original full-game unlock. Original purchases retain lifetime Pro. Unavailable ads never prevent restarting a level.

How does Thumb control work?

Slide the visible steering pad left or right to tilt the perch. Small moves near the middle make finer corrections. Lift your finger to level the perch; Pip may still drift, so a short opposite nudge helps slow it. At the end, follow “Keep Pip in the middle!” and hold Pip inside the glowing mint zone for two seconds.

My Game Center score has not appeared

Confirm that Game Center is signed in and reconnect to the internet. The game keeps the best pending score for each leaderboard and retries after authentication. Apple may take a short time to update its displays.

Does the game need an account or internet connection?

No Wobble Pop! account is required. Campaign play, progress, cosmetics, and Pass the Phone work offline. Purchases, restores, Game Center, and destinations chosen from the share sheet use Apple services and may require a connection.

How do I delete local progress?

Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to device backups and Apple’s platform behavior.

Accessibility and comfort

Thumb control is the full-featured alternative to Tilt. Settings also include sensitivity, Cozy difficulty, Reduced Motion support, sound, and haptics. Stop playing if device motion or the game causes discomfort.
