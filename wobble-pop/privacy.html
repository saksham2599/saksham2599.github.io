Wobble Pop! privacy policy

Effective date: `September 9, 2026`

Wobble Pop! is published by `saksham goyal` (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how the iPhone app handles information.

The short version

Wobble Pop! does not require an account. All levels are free, supported by occasional ads supplied by Google AdMob. Optional Pro removes ads. The app does not request cross-app tracking permission or send gameplay telemetry to our servers. Campaign progress and settings stay on your device.

Information handled on your device

  • Campaign progress, scores, badges, cosmetics, settings, and purchase-entitlement cache are stored locally so the game can work offline and restore your experience.
  • Pass the Phone names are used only for the current local match. They are not uploaded by Wobble Pop! and are not written into campaign progress.
  • Device-motion readings are used in real time for Tilt control. Wobble Pop! does not save or transmit raw motion readings. Thumb control is available as an alternative.
  • Share cards are rendered on the device. You choose whether and where to share them through Apple’s system share sheet.

Deleting Wobble Pop! removes app data stored in its local container, subject to your device backups and Apple’s platform behavior.

Apple services

Wobble Pop! can use Apple services that operate under Apple’s terms and privacy policy:

  • Game Center: When available, the app authenticates through Apple and can submit scores and achievement progress. Apple may display your Game Center name, scores, achievements, and related activity according to your Game Center settings. Wobble Pop! does not operate a separate Game Center profile database.
  • App Store and StoreKit: Purchases and restores are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment-card details. A local entitlement cache preserves verified ad-free Pro access and is refreshed against Apple's transaction history. Earlier full-game purchases include Pro.

Review Apple’s Privacy Policy and your Apple Account/Game Center settings for details about Apple’s processing.

Advertising partners

When ads are enabled and available online, Google AdMob may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device identifiers other than IDFA, diagnostic/performance information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. The app applies child-protective age treatment, general-audience content filtering, and non-personalized ad requests for all players. It uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices before requesting production ads. Required ad privacy choices appear in Settings. Pro stops new ad requests; previously processed data remains governed by Google's policies.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosures.

Developer telemetry

The current release has no telemetry server configured and does not send analytics or gameplay events to us. If we introduce optional analytics in a future version, we will first update this policy, the app’s consent experience, its privacy manifest, and the App Store privacy disclosures.

Children and families

Wobble Pop! is a general-audience, family-friendly game and is not submitted to Apple's Made for Kids category. Ad requests use child-protective settings for every player; third-party processing is described above. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us through a support channel, contact us so we can address it.

Security and retention

Because the current app does not operate a gameplay-data server, we do not retain gameplay information on our own servers. Local information remains on the device until the app or its data is removed. Scores and achievements submitted to Game Center are controlled through Apple’s services.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s features or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and App Store disclosures as required.
