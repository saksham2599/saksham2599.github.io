Your images are yours.

This policy covers the current Framewish development preview, previously named ImageRouter, and its private relay pilot. The developer and copyright owner is Saksham Goyal. The cloud service is not yet open for general customer onboarding.

On your device

Imported images stay in the app until you approve a cloud edit. The local sample runs on your device. The app creates a resized, pixel-only JPEG to remove the original EXIF and GPS metadata; faces, signs and visible locations remain part of the image.

When history is enabled, the app keeps up to 20 result images with a date, model label and sample indicator. It does not save your instruction text in history. History and privacy preferences use protected storage excluded from device backups. The history preference is remembered across launches. The relay address and access token are stored in device-only Keychain.

When you approve an AI edit

Your image and instruction pass over HTTPS through the configured relay to OpenAI to perform the edit. The relay also processes a random request ID and technical request information. The developer-operated pilot uses Cloudflare Workers and D1. The Worker does not write image or instruction content to persistent application storage. D1 stores random request IDs, attempt timestamps and a temporary active-request expiry to reduce duplicate and concurrent requests. Expired attempt rows are removed on a later edit after one hour; service backups may retain deleted records for longer.

Cloudflare processes connection and service metadata to operate and secure hosting. Application payload logging is disabled. OpenAI processes image content and instructions under its own data controls, including any applicable abuse-monitoring retention. Local deletion cannot remove records held by a provider. See Cloudflare privacy and OpenAI API data controls.

Provider credentials

The private Cloudflare pilot stores the tester’s provider key as an encrypted Worker secret, configured by the key owner. The relay can access it to make the requested edit. Provider keys are not embedded in the app or saved in its local preferences or Keychain. An optional private AWS relay supports enrollment into AWS Secrets Manager with KMS encryption; the app describes that route separately before enrollment. Only connect to a relay whose operator you trust.

Your controls and deletion

Clear the workspace to remove the current in-app images. Turn history off to prevent future saves, and use Delete all local history to remove existing history. Delete separately any copies saved to Photos or shared to another service. Disconnecting the device removes the saved relay handle, not the provider key. For Cloudflare, the key owner can delete or rotate secrets through Cloudflare and revoke the API key through OpenAI. The optional AWS relay provides its own vault deletion action. There is no Framewish customer account in this preview.

Analytics, advertising and training

The current app sends no product analytics and contains no advertising SDK. The developer does not use your photos or instructions for model training, sell them, or collect them for model evaluation. Hosting and provider processing described above still applies; this is not a promise that no technical data leaves the device.

Support and policy changes

If you contact the developer, the contact service and developer receive the details you choose to send for handling that request. Send only information needed to explain the issue. This policy will be updated to match material changes before new account, provider or analytics features are released.

Contact the developer

Framewish is developed by Saksham Goyal. For support or a privacy request, use the contact details on Saksham Goyal’s developer page. Include “Framewish” and your app version. Never send an API key, relay token, password, or personal photo in a support message.
